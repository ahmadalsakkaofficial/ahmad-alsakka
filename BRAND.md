# Ahmad Alsakka — Brand palette (Nov 2026)

Black + bright gold. Use for the site, ads, carousels, thumbnails, videos and emails.

| Role | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background (black) | `#070707` | 7, 7, 7 | Page / slide / video background |
| Panel | `#141414` | 20, 20, 20 | Cards, boxes, sections |
| Panel 2 | `#181818` | 24, 24, 24 | Alternate section background |
| Gold (primary accent) | `#F5B942` | 245, 185, 66 | Buttons, highlight bar behind key words, numbers, icons |
| Gold light (glow) | `#FFD06E` | 255, 208, 110 | Glows, gradients, hover light |
| Cream (text) | `#F2EEE6` | 242, 238, 230 | Headlines and body text on black |
| Muted text | `#A39E95` | 163, 158, 149 | Secondary text, captions |

Rules
- Text on gold is always black `#070707`, never white.
- One gold highlight per headline (marker bar behind 1–3 words).
- Glow = radial gradient of gold at 20–55% opacity behind the subject.
- Fonts: Thmanyah Serif Display (all Arabic: Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700, Black 900), Inter for Latin/numbers. Files in public/fonts.
